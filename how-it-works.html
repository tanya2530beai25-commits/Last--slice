How LastSlice Works

Connecting you with nearby food deals before they go to waste — in 3 simple steps.

👤 For Customers

Save money on great food from local shops — takes less than a minute.

1
📍

Browse deals near you

Open LastSlice and see live flash deals from bakeries and cafes nearby. Each card shows the item, price, discount %, distance, and how many are left.

2
🛒

Click "Claim Deal" and fill your details

When you see a deal you want, tap "Claim Deal". You'll fill in your name, phone, pickup time, and payment method. Takes about 30 seconds.

3
🎟

Get your Order ID

After placing the order you'll get a unique Order ID (e.g. LS-482910). This is your reservation confirmation — screenshot it or save it.

4
🎉

Pick up & enjoy!

Head to the shop before the deal expires. Show your Order ID to the staff, pay the deal price, and walk away with great food at a fraction of the cost.

🏪 For Shops & Bakeries

Sell surplus food instead of throwing it away — earn more every day.

1
📋

Post a deal in seconds

Click "Post a Deal". Fill in your shop name, item, original price, deal price, quantity, and expiry time. It goes live instantly.

2
📱

Customers claim your deal

Nearby customers see your deal and claim it. You'll know how many orders are placed and can prepare accordingly.

3
💸

Serve & earn

Customers arrive, show their Order ID, and pay. You get revenue from food that would otherwise go to waste — and build loyal local customers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is LastSlice free to use? ▼
Yes! LastSlice is completely free for customers. No subscription fees, booking fees, or hidden charges. You only pay the deal price directly to the shop.
What if I claim a deal but can't pick it up? ▼
Contact the shop directly if you can't make it. Since these are surplus food deals, no-shows waste the food we're trying to save — so only claim deals you plan to pick up.
How do I pay — online or at the shop? ▼
You pay directly at the shop — in cash, UPI, or card as you prefer. LastSlice does not handle any payments. This keeps things simple and the money goes straight to the shop.
How are deals different from normal shop prices? ▼
LastSlice deals are specifically for surplus or end-of-day food that would otherwise go unsold. Shops offer them at 30–60% off because they'd rather earn something than waste the food entirely.
