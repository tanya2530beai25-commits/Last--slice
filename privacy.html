🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's exactly what we collect and why.

📅 Last updated: April 2026

LastSlice ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard data when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or use LastSlice, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number (provided when placing an order)
  • Pickup time preferences and special instructions
  • General location (to show nearby deals — we do not track your precise GPS)
  • Usage data such as which deals you viewed or claimed

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process your deal reservation and generate an Order ID
  • Send your details to the relevant shop so they can prepare your order
  • Improve our platform and personalise deal recommendations
  • Communicate important updates or changes to our service

3. What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties
  • We do not run ads or share data with advertisers
  • We do not store payment information — all payments happen directly at the shop
  • We do not track your precise location without permission

4. Data Sharing

Your order details (name, phone, pickup time) are shared only with the specific shop whose deal you claimed. We do not share your data with any other third parties unless required by law.

5. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorised access or disclosure. Data is stored securely and retained only as long as necessary to provide our service.

6. Your Rights

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete your personal information
  • Opt out of any marketing communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@lastslice.in.

7. Cookies

LastSlice uses minimal, functional cookies to remember your filter preferences and session state. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of LastSlice after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions, please reach out via our contact page or email hello@lastslice.in.
