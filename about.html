🍕 About LastSlice

Our Mission

To connect people with great surplus food from local shops — reducing waste, saving money, and supporting independent businesses, one deal at a time.

How It All Started

LastSlice was born in Chandigarh when we noticed how much perfectly good food gets thrown away at the end of the day. Bakeries discarding fresh croissants. Cafes pouring out coffee. All because closing time had arrived.

We thought — what if we could connect those shops with nearby people who'd love a great deal? A win for the shop, a win for the customer, and a win for the planet.

That's LastSlice. Simple, local, and real.
